Frames: *origin* has the radar at (0, 0) as in this project; *offset* puts it at (+200 km, +200 km) through Stone Soup's `translation_offset`, so that every position coordinate is positive. Stone Soup is unmodified in both.

Cause of the origin-frame difference: `stonesoup.functions.jacobian` takes the measurement Jacobian by forward differences with step `1e8 * np.spacing(x)`, then replaces every step below 1e-8 by 1e-8.
